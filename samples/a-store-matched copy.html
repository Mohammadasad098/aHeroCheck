The formula

Three peptides, one bottle

Each milliliter is mixed for research protocols that stack tissue support, mobility work, and copper-peptide signaling.

01 - BPC-157

Tissue research

The pentadecapeptide used in gut, tendon, and recovery models. Here it sits in the same protocol as TB-500 instead of a second vial.

02 - TB-500

Mobility research

Thymosin beta-4 fragment studied for actin regulation and recovery windows. Paired so daily handling stays simple.

03 - GHK-CU

Copper peptide

The copper-binding tripeptide associated with repair and skin-quality research - rounded out with Tropical Punch flavoring.

The protocol

Built for a 30-day run

One serving marks one day on the label. Adjust only to the protocol you already run.

Step 01

Measure the serving

Each bottle is labeled at 10 mg/ml across 30 ml. Log the volume your protocol calls for before you start the bottle.

Step 02

Stay consistent

Thirty servings are mapped to a month of daily research use. Keep handling notes with the lot, not in a separate spreadsheet you will lose.

Step 03

Store as labeled

Follow the carton: cool, dark, and upright. Flavor is Tropical Punch; storage is still peptide storage.
